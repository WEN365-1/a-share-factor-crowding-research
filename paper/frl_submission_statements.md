## Data availability statement

The daily market and valuation data used in this study are obtained from Baostock, and the individual-stock margin-financing data are obtained from public disclosures by the Shanghai and Shenzhen Stock Exchanges. The analysis code, configuration files, and manuscript materials are available at https://github.com/WEN365-1/a-share-factor-crowding-research. No stand-alone raw, security-level, aggregate, or other provider-derived datasets are redistributed while asset-specific redistribution permission remains unresolved. Researchers must obtain the upstream inputs under the providers' terms and construct the documented analysis inputs themselves; the public package alone does not reproduce the empirical results.
