## Data availability statement

The daily market and valuation data used in this study are obtained from Baostock, and the exchange-level margin-financing data are obtained from the Shanghai and Shenzhen Stock Exchanges through publicly accessible interfaces. Redistribution of the raw provider data may be subject to the providers' terms. The analysis code, configuration files, derived-data documentation, evidence manifest, run manifest, and derived summary outputs are publicly available at https://github.com/WEN365-1/a-share-factor-crowding-research. Raw data are documented in a companion repository (https://github.com/WEN365-1/a-share-factor-crowding-raw-data) and should be retrieved from the upstream providers using the documented data route.
